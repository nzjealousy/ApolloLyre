The tools to manage and understand your listening habits.

Apollo Lyre addresses three issues faced by music listeners.

📊

Limited overview of music collection statistics

⏱️

Personalized playlists take effort and time to build

🗂️

Difficulty in organising an accumulated music collection

Everything your library needs

Three main modules working together with your Spotify account.

🎧

Track, Library and Playlist Management

Search songs by title or artist, save them to your music library and add or remove personal tags. Create, rename, reorder and delete playlists, and add or remove songs in them.

👤

User Management

Register an account, log in or out, connect or disconnect your Spotify account, view and update your profile, and delete your account whenever you want.

📈

Listening Analytics

View your listening history and your analytics dashboard: total songs played, total listening hours, most and least played songs and artists, and top artists and tracks.

Get started in four steps

No complicated setup. No extra statistics services.

Sign up

Create your Apollo Lyre account and log in.

Connect Spotify

Authorise access with your own consent.

Organise

Save and tag songs, and manage your playlists.

Discover

Open your analytics and explore your habits.

Your data, treated with care

Ethical and responsible data use is built in from day one.

  • Consent first. Apollo Lyre never sees your Spotify password. You approve access on Spotify's own page.
  • Hashed passwords. Your password is stored in hashed form, never in plain text.
  • You're in control. Update your profile or delete your account and its stored data anytime.
  • Follows Spotify's terms. Works only within the data the official API allows.
